# Tesla Roadster (2nd gen, revealed 2026-10-15) — compiled data

Unveiled Oct 15 2026 at SpaceX McGregor, TX ("Tesla Live"). Production expected 2027–2028 at Giga Texas. $50,000 reservations reopened; price not announced.

## Trims (Tesla's figures)
| | Performance | Thrust | Rocket |
|---|---|---|---|
| Purpose | Road ready | Race ready | Air ready |
| Seats | 2 | 2 | 1 (tanks take passenger space) |
| 0-60 mph | < 1.6 s | < 0.9 s | < 0.59 s |
| Thrusters | none | rear cold-gas thrusters (4 rear ports, ~80 kN shown) | perimeter thrusters for forward + upward thrust and rotation ("So yes, it can fly") |
| Availability | Reservation | Limited release | Invite only, not road legal |

- Top speed: > 230 mph (~370 km/h), Rocket trim
- Range: > 300 mi (the 2017 promise was 620 mi)
- Cornering: ~2 g (Tesla claim)
- Downforce: 4,700 kg on Tesla's display (outlets doubt it). Multi-mode fan suction system with adjustable sealing skirts; can hold the car upside down while stationary
- Active rear wing with several positions. Track Mode lowers ride height
- Steer-by-wire yoke, manual controls, fixed seat
- Magnesium, titanium and carbon fiber construction. Mass not given; estimated about 1,700–1,900 kg
- Demo runs: 0-60 in 1.0 s, then 0.81 s on video
- Stunts shown: jump off a ramp, kickflip over a Tesla Semi and a row of cars, thruster jets leaving white vapor clouds, drifting and burnouts in a ring-shaped arena with red light strips

## Exterior design (from reference photos in this folder)
- Cybertruck-style faceted wedge. Low, long nose with a sharp leading edge and hidden headlights (a thin LED slit). Roofline rises steeply from the nose to a peak over the cabin, then a long fastback slopes to a flat Kammback tail
- Two-tone: Ano White (satin pearl white) upper body. Gloss black/charcoal lower body and rocker band, which rises in a diagonal crease from the front wheel toward the rear wheel
- Butterfly/scissor doors hinged at the A-pillar, opening up and forward. Dark gold-tinted glass. Black glass roof canopy that flows into a black rear glass section
- Thin, angular side window with a sharp rear kink. Large triangular rear-quarter window behind the door
- Rear: full-width red light bar plus a second thin red line below it, a white plate panel ("RDSTR"), and an aggressive black diffuser with vertical fins. **Four round thruster nozzles** in a row (bronze/copper rings) under the plate. Small vertical air outlets at the rear corners
- Rear wing (Thrust/Rocket): a large swan-neck wing on two struts, with angled white endplates that sweep up at the tips. Mounted high above the tail
- Wheels: thin-spoke turbine/star design (about 6 twisted split spokes) in satin silver or black with red accents. Smooth flat aero covers are also shown. Low-profile tires
- Small red side marker on the rear fender, small amber marker on the front fender
- Estimated dimensions: length 4.4 m, width 2.0 m, height 1.15 m, wheelbase 2.65 m, wheel diameter about 0.72 m
